A WinForms application for a computer-graphics course. Closed polygons are built from straight edges, cubic Bézier curves, and circular arcs. Vertices, Bézier control points, and whole polygons can be dragged. Length and parallel constraints are enforced while editing, and continuity at a shared vertex can be G0, G1, or C1. Each edge is drawn by one of three strategies: the library rasterizer, Bresenham's line algorithm, or Xiaolin Wu antialiasing, at a chosen stroke width. A scene can be saved and loaded as JSON.
